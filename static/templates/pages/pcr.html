How to Use PCR

  1. Choose a credential list at the top.
  2. Enter credentials in username,password format (one per line).
  3. Click Submit Changes to save your changes.

Enter one credential per line in this format:

username,password

Examples:

administrator,MyNewP@ss123
john.doe,SecurePassword!
"quoted_user","quoted,password"

Tip: Enable "Strip surrounding quotes from values" to automatically remove outer quotes.

The Reset to Defaults button restores all credentials in the selected list to their original values.
